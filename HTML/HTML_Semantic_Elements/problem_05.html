Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a large educational and career event designed for students, graduates and job seekers.

Visitors can meet representatives from universities, colleges, training institutes and leading organizations.

The event provides opportunities to explore higher education, internships, placements, scholarships and career pathways.

Registration is required for entry.

Date: 20–21 November 2026

Venue: National Convention Centre, New Delhi

Event Objectives

The main objective is to connect attendees with reliable education and employment opportunities.

Students can compare courses, understand eligibility requirements and speak directly with participating organizations.

Job seekers can learn about internships, placements and professional development opportunities.

The fair also promotes informed career decisions through expert guidance and networking.

Career Fair Terminology

Career Fair
An event where organizations and career seekers connect to discuss employment and education opportunities.
Internship
A temporary learning or work opportunity that provides practical experience.
Placement
Assistance or an opportunity that connects candidates with potential employers.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students for educational purposes.
Apprenticeship
A structured learning program combining practical workplace experience with training.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can attend the fair?

Students, graduates, job seekers and anyone exploring education or career opportunities can attend.

Is registration required?

Yes. Attendees should register before visiting the event.

What should I bring?

Carry identification, an updated resume and relevant educational documents.

Can I meet employers directly?

Yes. Participating organizations will have dedicated booths and representatives.
