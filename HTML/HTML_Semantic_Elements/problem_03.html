About CityCare Clinic

CityCare Multispecialty Clinic provides accessible and patient-focused healthcare services across multiple medical departments.

Our experienced doctors and healthcare professionals work together to provide safe, professional and compassionate medical care.

Clinic timings are generally from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.
