TechPro X15 Pro Laptop

The TechPro X15 Pro is a powerful and lightweight laptop designed for students, professionals and creators.

It combines a high-performance processor, fast SSD storage, a bright display and long-lasting battery in a modern design.

Technical Information

The laptop is available with different combinations of processor, RAM and storage.

Recommended configuration: 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD and a high-performance processor.

Processor : Intel Core Ultra RAM : 16 GB DDR5 Storage : 512 GB NVMe SSD Display : 15.6 inch Full HD Graphics : Integrated Graphics Wi-Fi : Wi-Fi 6 USB : USB-C + USB-A

Display refresh rate can reach 120 Hz on supported configurations.

Storage capacity may be expanded depending on the selected configuration.
