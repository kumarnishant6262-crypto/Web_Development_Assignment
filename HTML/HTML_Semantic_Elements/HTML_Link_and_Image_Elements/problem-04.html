Agra Travel Destination Guide

Explore the historical beauty, culture and food of Agra

1. Famous Attractions

Agra is one of India's most famous tourist destinations. The city is best known for the magnificent Taj Mahal, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Other important attractions include Agra Fort and Mehtab Bagh. These places provide visitors with a glimpse into India's rich Mughal history.

2. Culture and History

Agra has a rich Mughal heritage. The city became an important center during the Mughal period and contains magnificent buildings, gardens and monuments.

Visitors can experience traditional handicrafts, marble inlay work and local markets. The city's architecture reflects a mixture of Persian, Indian and Islamic influences.

Read more about Agra's heritage on the official Incredible India website .

3. Famous Food

Agra is famous for its delicious Mughlai cuisine. Visitors should try local specialties such as petha, a famous sweet from Agra.

Other popular choices include Mughlai biryani, kebabs, parathas and traditional North Indian dishes.

4. Best Time to Visit

The most comfortable period for visiting Agra is generally from October to March because the weather is cooler.

Recommended Season: October to March

Winter mornings can be cool, so visitors should carry suitable clothing when visiting early in the day.

5. Travel Tips

Important Travel Tip: Keep your valuables safe and follow the official entry rules at monuments.

Plan monument visits in advance, carry drinking water, wear comfortable footwear and keep some extra time for traffic around popular tourist areas.

For information about the Taj Mahal, visit the official Taj Mahal website .
