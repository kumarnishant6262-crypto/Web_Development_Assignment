Breaking News: College Launches New Digital Media Center

Students Get a New Space for Journalism, Technology and Creativity

The college has officially opened a new Digital Media Center to support students interested in journalism, photography, video production and online media. The new facility was inaugurated on 1 September 2026 at the college campus.

The center is located in the main academic building and includes modern computers, cameras and editing facilities. Students can use the space to create news reports, podcasts, interviews and other digital projects. The facility is expected to become an important part of the college's media activities.

According to the college media club, the main purpose of the center is to give students practical experience in journalism and digital communication. Students will be encouraged to report campus events and important community stories. Faculty members will also guide students during different media projects.

The college expects the new center to increase student participation in media activities. More than 500 students are expected to benefit from the facility during the academic year. The media club said that the center will help students develop communication, research and technical skills.

The launch event was attended by students, teachers and members of the college administration. The event included demonstrations of video editing, photography and digital publishing. Officials said the project represents an important step toward creating a modern learning environment.
