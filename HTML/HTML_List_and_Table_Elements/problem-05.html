Fest Introduction

The Annual College Fest 2026 brings students together to showcase their creativity, talent and teamwork. The event will be conducted over three days at different venues across the college campus.

Fest Rules

1. All participants must carry their college ID card. 2. Participants must report to the venue before the scheduled time. 3. Students must maintain discipline during all events. 4. The decision of the judging panel will be final. 5. Participants must follow instructions given by event coordinators. 6. Any form of misconduct may result in disqualification.

Important Terms

Open Event: An event that is open to all eligible students. Team Event: A competition in which participants compete as a group. Individual Event: A competition in which one participant competes individually. Registration: The process of officially entering a student into a fest event. Coordinator: A person responsible for managing a particular event. Final Round: The last stage of a competition where selected participants compete.
