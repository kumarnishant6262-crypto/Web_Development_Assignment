Spice Garden Restaurant

Welcome to Spice Garden Restaurant, where delicious food, fresh ingredients and a memorable dining experience come together.

Starters

Paneer Tikka - Grilled Indian cottage cheese with spices. Vegetable Spring Rolls - Crispy rolls filled with fresh vegetables. Chicken Tikka - Tender grilled chicken marinated with Indian spices.

Main Course

Butter Chicken - Creamy tomato-based chicken curry. Paneer Butter Masala - Soft paneer cooked in a rich gravy. Vegetable Biryani - Aromatic rice cooked with vegetables and spices. Dal Makhani - Slow-cooked black lentils with butter and cream.

Desserts

Gulab Jamun - Soft sweet dumplings served with sugar syrup. Chocolate Brownie - Warm chocolate brownie served as a sweet treat. Ice Cream - Creamy vanilla ice cream.

Beverages

Mango Lassi - Refreshing yogurt drink blended with mango. Fresh Lime Soda - Refreshing lime drink. Masala Tea - Traditional Indian tea with aromatic spices.

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

1. Begin with Paneer Tikka and Vegetable Spring Rolls.
2. Enjoy our signature Butter Chicken or Paneer Butter Masala.
3. Add fragrant Vegetable Biryani and Dal Makhani.
4. Finish your meal with Gulab Jamun or Chocolate Brownie.
5. Enjoy a refreshing Mango Lassi or Masala Tea.

Important Menu Terms

Vegan: Food prepared without animal products. Chef's Special: A specially selected dish recommended by our chef. Gluten-Free: Food prepared without gluten-containing ingredients. Bestseller: One of the most popular dishes among our customers. Combo: A combination of multiple food items offered together.
