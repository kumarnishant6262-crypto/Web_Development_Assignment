Practical Class Procedure

Step 1: Read the practical instructions before entering the laboratory. Step 2: Bring the required notebook and study material. Step 3: Attend the practical class on time. Step 4: Complete the assigned practical task. Step 5: Submit the practical work to the faculty member.

Academic Terms

Lecture: A teaching session in which a faculty member explains academic concepts. Lab: A practical session where students perform experiments or programming tasks. Tutorial: A smaller academic session focused on practice and problem solving. Internal Assessment: Evaluation conducted during the academic semester. Practical: A hands-on academic activity designed to develop practical skills.

Academic Rules

1. Students must attend classes regularly.2. Students must carry their college ID card.3. Students must maintain discipline inside the classroom.4. Assignments must be submitted before the deadline.5. Students must follow laboratory safety instructions.
