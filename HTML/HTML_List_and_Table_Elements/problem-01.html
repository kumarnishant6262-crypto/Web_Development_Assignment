ABC University - University Admission Handbook

Admission Overview

Welcome to ABC University. This handbook provides important information about the admission procedure, eligibility requirements, documents, fees and important admission terms.

Admission Office Message: We welcome all students to ABC University. Please read the admission guidelines carefully and complete every required step before the deadline.

Application Deadline: 30 June 2026

Eligibility

Academic Qualification: Candidates must have passed the required qualifying examination. Minimum Marks: Candidates must meet the minimum marks specified by the university. Age Requirement: Candidates must satisfy the age criteria applicable to the selected program.

Application Process

Step 1: Visit the official university admission website.
Step 2: Read the admission instructions carefully.
Step 3: Complete the online application.
Step 4: Upload the required documents.
Step 5: Pay the applicable application fee.
Step 6: Submit the application and save the confirmation.

Required Documents

• Class 10 Marksheet
• Class 12 Marksheet
• Transfer Certificate
• Character Certificate
• Passport-size Photograph
• Government-issued Identity Proof

Important Terms

Eligibility: The conditions a student must satisfy to apply for admission. Merit List: A list of candidates prepared according to their qualifying performance. Counselling: The process through which eligible students select courses or seats. Scholarship: Financial assistance provided to eligible students. Registration: The process of officially registering for admission.
