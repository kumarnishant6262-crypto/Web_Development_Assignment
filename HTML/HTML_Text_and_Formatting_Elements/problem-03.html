Heavy Rain Disrupts Delhi-NCR

Heavy rain and thunderstorms affected several parts of Delhi-NCR today, causing waterlogging and traffic congestion. The bad weather also affected flight operations at Indira Gandhi International Airport.

At least 15 Delhi-bound flights were diverted. Twelve flights were diverted to Jaipur, two to Chandigarh and one to Lucknow. Several other flights were delayed or cancelled because of the weather conditions.

Airlines including IndiGo, Air India and SpiceJet advised passengers to check their flight status before travelling to the airport. The weather also brought thunderstorms, lightning and strong winds in parts of the capital.

Heavy rain caused major disruptions to Delhi's flights and road traffic.

Humanoid Robot Beats Usain Bolt's 100m Record

Chinese robot clocks an incredible 9.39 seconds

A Chinese humanoid robot has made headlines after breaking the men's 100-metre world record at the World Humanoid Robot Games in Beijing. The robot, named Tiangong Ultra, completed the race in just 9.39 seconds, faster than Usain Bolt's human record of 9.58 seconds set in 2009.

The record-breaking performance took place during a preliminary heat. Another humanoid robot, Lightning, also finished the 100-metre race faster than Bolt's record, recording a time of 9.47 seconds. The achievement has attracted worldwide attention and highlighted the rapid development of humanoid robotics.

The World Humanoid Robot Games feature more than 2,000 robots competing in events such as sprinting, football and table tennis. The event demonstrates how quickly robots are improving in movement, balance and physical abilities. This achievement could mark an important moment in the development of advanced robotics.
