Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must carry their college ID card at all times while on campus.

Students must maintain cleanliness in classrooms and common areas.
Please use the designated dustbins for all waste.

Attendance

Students must maintain at least 60% attendance to be eligible for examinations.

Students falling below the required attendance percentage may be barred from appearing in the examination.

Examination

Students must reach the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the scheduled time.

Mobile phones and other unauthorized electronic devices are not permitted during examinations.
Students should carry only the permitted stationery and materials.

Assignment Submission

All assignments must be submitted before the given deadline.

Assignments submitted late may receive a penalty or reduced marks.

Students should follow the prescribed format and submission instructions provided by the faculty.

Discipline

Students must behave respectfully towards faculty members, staff, and fellow students.

Misconduct, bullying, or damage to college property may result in disciplinary action.
