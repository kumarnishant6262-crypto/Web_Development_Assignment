Science Notes

Chemistry

Water: H2O

Carbon Dioxide: CO2

Oxygen: O2

Sulfuric Acid: H2SO4

Glucose: C6H12O6

Definition:

An element is a pure substance made up of only one type of atom.

A compound is pure substance made of two or more different elements joined by chemical bonds.

Physics

Area is measured in m2.

Volume is measured in m3.

Speed = Distance / Time

Einstein's mass-energy equation: E = mc2

One kilometre = 103 metres.

Important: Force is measured in Newtons (N).

Biology

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their food using sunlight.

Carbon dioxide + Water => Glucose + Oxygen

Definition: The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

Chlorophyll is the green pigment present in plants that helps absorb sunlight.
