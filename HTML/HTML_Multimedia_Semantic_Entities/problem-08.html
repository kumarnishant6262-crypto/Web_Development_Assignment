Personal Finance Basics

Saving and Budgeting

Track income and expenses, maintain an emergency fund and compare financial products before choosing one.

Important: Financial education is general information, not personal financial advice.

“A simple budget can make your financial goals easier to understand and manage.”

Financial Tips

  • Track monthly spending.
  • Save regularly.
  • Compare interest rates and charges.
  • Read terms before signing.
  • Protect banking credentials.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.

Account-Opening Steps

  1. Choose an account type.
  2. Compare fees and benefits.
  3. Prepare required documents.
  4. Complete the application.
  5. Verify your identity.
  6. Activate the account.
